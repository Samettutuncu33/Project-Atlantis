How the simulation works and what is assumed
  • Accounts A and C size positions to a 10% annual volatility target. At $200 most targets fall below Saxo's 1,000-unit minimum and round to zero, so these accounts may never trade.
  • Accounts B and D trade exactly the 1,000-unit minimum ticket whenever there is a signal. That is far more risk than the volatility target: roughly 20× leverage with four pairs open.
  • Trend 12m: long if the pair rose over the last 12 months, short if it fell; re-sized monthly (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012). Breakout 55/20: enter on a 55-day high or low, exit on the opposite 20-day channel, 2× ATR stop.
  • Saxo costs used: $1 minimum commission per FX ticket, 0.75% p.a. Tom/Next mark-up, 2% p.a. on unrealised P&L, 0.25% currency conversion. Spreads are Dukascopy's real bid/ask because Saxo's are not public.
  • Every run replays all accounts from the live start and confirms that each earlier fill is reproduced exactly. The ledger is reconciled against an independent recomputation every run.
  • Retail margin per ESMA (3.33% majors, 5% AUD pairs), close-out at 50% margin, negative balance protection.
